# Decision log: template

> The project's memory of *why*. Append only; never rewrite history.
>
> Without this, the next session re-proposes what you already rejected, with
> confidence.

---

## <Date>, <Short title of the decision>

**What was decided.** <One or two sentences.>

**Why.** <The reason, including the constraint that forced it.>

**What was rejected, and why.** <The obvious alternative, and what made it
wrong here. This is the part that stops the discussion from reopening.>

**What would change this.** <The condition under which this decision should
be revisited. Often "if <X> becomes true".>
